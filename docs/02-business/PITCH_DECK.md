# Usly Pitch Deck — Narrative Draft

## 1. Title
**Usly** — Turn one weekly check-in into one real shared experience.

## 2. Problem
Couples care about their relationship but shared time is often lost to fatigue, budget uncertainty, repetitive planning, and unequal mental load.

## 3. Insight
Couples do not need another chat app or endless relationship content. They need a lightweight ritual that converts both partners' current context into an achievable plan.

## 4. Solution
A private weekly sync captures energy, need, time, and budget. Usly returns three relevant experiences. Both partners vote, schedule one, complete it, and provide lightweight feedback.

## 5. Why now
- Couples increasingly coordinate life through digital tools.
- Existing products are fragmented across content, calendars, memories, and messaging.
- Cost-sensitive, localized experience planning is underserved in Persian-language markets.
- A curated rule-based MVP is now inexpensive enough for a solo founder to test.

## 6. Initial market
Working urban couples aged 25–38, together 1+ years, who experience recurring planning friction and want intentional shared time without therapy framing.

## 7. Product wedge
Own the weekly planning moment before expanding into memories, paid packs, local experiences, and commerce.

## 8. Business model
- Freemium core loop
- Annual Couple Plus subscription
- One-time themed packs
- Later: commission on experiences, vouchers, gifts, and expert packs

## 9. Competitive landscape
- Relationship-content apps: strong content, weaker real-world action.
- Couple calendars: strong coordination, weaker emotional context.
- Private couple spaces: strong memories/messages, weaker planning outcome.
- Generic calendars/messengers: convenient but not designed for mutual weekly decisions.

## 10. Defensibility
Localized experience inventory, bilateral preference history, trust, privacy, culturally appropriate content, and data linking context to completed experiences.

## 11. Validation plan
Manual four-week pilot with 20 couples. Success requires bilateral week-four retention, completed experiences, low pressure reports, and demonstrated willingness to pay.

## 12. Roadmap
Validation → MVP → paid beta → one-city marketplace → privacy-preserving personalization → international localization.

## 13. Ask
Initial pilot couples, relationship-content advisors, product design feedback, and later local experience partners.

## 14. Founder fit
Founder has product ownership, backend engineering, architecture, and experience building consumer digital-health products, with direct understanding of financial and scheduling pressures in married life.
