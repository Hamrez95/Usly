# Product Strategy

## Strategic position
Usly is not a generic couples app. It is a weekly shared-experience operating loop for busy couples.

## Wedge
The first wedge is the Thursday-to-weekend planning moment:
- both partners have limited time;
- neither wants a long conversation;
- budget and energy vary;
- they want a concrete plan, not an article.

## Differentiation
- Converts two private inputs into one actionable shared plan.
- Localized for Persian language, Solar Hijri dates, Iranian holidays, budget sensitivity, and locally realistic activities.
- Measures completed experiences, not content views.
- Neutral language; never judges the relationship or either partner.

## Initial user persona
### Primary
Dual-income or busy couples, 25–38, relationship duration 1+ years, urban, smartphone-comfortable, with recurring weekend planning friction.

### Excluded from initial positioning
- Couples in active crisis or abuse situations
- Users seeking diagnosis or therapy
- New dating discovery
- Families primarily seeking household task management

## North-star metric
Weekly Active Couples Completing a Shared Experience (WACSE).

## Supporting metrics
- Partner invitation acceptance rate
- Couple activation rate: both complete first sync
- Week-4 retained couples
- Suggested experience selection rate
- Selected-to-completed experience conversion
- Mutual participation rate
- “Would miss Usly” score
- Paid conversion and package purchase rate
- Pressure/harm report rate

## Go/no-go gates
### Validation gate
Proceed to MVP only when at least 20 couples complete a manual four-week pilot and:
- 70%+ partner invitations accepted
- 50%+ couples active in week four
- 40%+ selected experiences completed
- 30%+ say they would miss Usly
- 15%+ show payment intent or pre-purchase
- under 10% report pressure or conflict caused by the workflow

### MVP gate
Proceed to paid beta only when retention is bilateral, not driven by one partner.

### Scale gate
Do not add marketplace, AI, finance, or therapy-adjacent features until the core weekly loop demonstrates repeat usage.

## Monetization strategy
1. Free core loop with limits.
2. Couple Plus annual subscription.
3. One-time themed packs: anniversary, travel, money conversation, at-home dates.
4. Later: commerce commission on bookable experiences and gifts.

## Moat candidates
- Couple-specific explicit preference history
- Local experience catalog and partner network
- Trust and privacy brand
- High-quality Persian interaction content
- Behavioral data connecting context to completed positive experiences

## Strategic anti-goals
- Chasing daily engagement at the cost of pressure
- Maximizing notification volume
- Building a broad super-app before retention proof
- Selling sensitive relationship data or using it for advertising
