# Feature Priority

## P0 — Validation before coding
1. Recruit 20 pilot couples.
2. Run four weekly syncs manually.
3. Curate 60–100 activity cards across energy, time, budget, and setting.
4. Measure bilateral participation, selection, completion, and payment intent.
5. Interview both partners separately after week four.

## P1 — MVP core
1. Account creation and secure sign-in.
2. Solo onboarding and preference capture.
3. Partner invitation, consent, pairing, and unpairing.
4. Weekly sync with three private inputs: energy, need, time/budget.
5. Reveal neutral shared context only after both respond.
6. Rule-based experience recommendation.
7. Three options: easy, balanced, different.
8. Independent voting and shared selection.
9. Add selected experience to device calendar.
10. Gentle reminders with privacy-safe notification text.
11. Mark experience completed or skipped.
12. One-question feedback and optional private note.
13. Four-week history.
14. Skip and pause controls.
15. Account export and deletion.

## P2 — Paid beta
1. Hidden-answer weekly question.
2. Experience favorites and couple wish list.
3. Themed activity packs.
4. Memory card: one photo, one sentence, repeat-worthiness.
5. Better recommendation rules from explicit feedback.
6. Couple Plus subscription and entitlement handling.
7. Admin content panel.
8. Safety reporting and content moderation workflow.
9. Basic product analytics with no sensitive free-text capture.

## P3 — Product-market fit
1. Persian and English localization.
2. Solar Hijri calendar support.
3. City-aware suggestions using curated local data.
4. At-home, free, low-budget, no-car, and short-time filters.
5. Anniversary and travel packs.
6. Shared lightweight goals without money custody.
7. Referral system.
8. Experiment framework for onboarding and reminders.
9. Support center and in-app feedback.

## P4 — Growth
1. Experience marketplace in one city.
2. Merchant portal and offer management.
3. Voucher and reservation integration.
4. Gift recommendations and affiliate commerce.
5. Creator/expert content packs with review workflow.
6. Couple subscription gifting.
7. Advanced lifecycle messaging.

## P5 — Advanced platform
1. Optional AI-assisted recommendation constrained to curated inventory.
2. Explainable recommendations; no diagnosis or conflict arbitration.
3. Context-aware trip and date planning.
4. Privacy-preserving personalization.
5. Partner ecosystem APIs.
6. Multi-region infrastructure and international rollout.

## Never build without a separate safety review
- Relationship health score
- Partner performance score
- Therapy or diagnosis claims
- Open-ended AI counselor
- Covert location or activity tracking
- Read receipts, last-seen pressure mechanics, or forced streaks
- Public profiles or social feed
- Advertising based on sensitive relationship data
